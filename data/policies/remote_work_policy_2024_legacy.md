# Northstar Cloud Corp. — Remote Work Guidelines

**Policy ID:** POL-RW-2024  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024 (Version 1.2)  
**Owner:** Human Resources

## Section 1.1: Purpose and Eligibility
Northstar Cloud Corp. provides structured hybrid work options designed to preserve strong office culture, cross-team alignment, and spontaneous mentorship. Full-time salaried employees become eligible for hybrid scheduling after completing a 90-day probationary period with no active performance improvement plan on file. Part-time employees, temporary staff, interns, and laboratory operations personnel are required to work on-site five days per week unless granted a formal medical accommodation by Human Resources in consultation with their department director.

## Section 1.2: Weekly Remote Work Cadence (Code: RW-101)
Eligible employees are permitted to work remotely up to **two (2) days per week**, with Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays designated as mandatory in-office attendance days across all regional hubs. Fridays and Wednesdays may be selected as remote workdays subject to manager sign-off at the beginning of each calendar quarter. Employees must log remote workdays in the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance. Permanent remote work exceptions are strictly limited to field sales engineers and require written authorization from the Chief Operating Officer.

## Section 1.3: Core Collaboration Hours and Availability (Code: RW-102)
While working from a home office, employees must remain online and actively available during **core business hours from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)** regardless of their local regional office location. Calendar status must reflect focus blocks, client calls, and lunch breaks accurately throughout the workday. Employees are expected to answer Slack pings within 15 minutes during core hours and join all engineering standups from a quiet, dedicated workspace free of household or background distractions.

## Section 1.4: Home Internet Stipend (Code: RW-NET-50)
Northstar Cloud Corp. provides a flat **$50 per month** home internet reimbursement to hybrid employees who work at least one scheduled day per week from home. Employees must upload their itemized monthly ISP invoice to ConcurFlow under expense code `RW-NET-50` within 15 business days of the billing statement date. Late submissions will not be reimbursed retroactively. Mobile hotspot data plans, satellite add-ons, and bundled cable television packages are excluded from reimbursement under this policy.

## Section 1.5: Home Office Equipment Allowance (Code: RW-ERG-400)
New regular employees are eligible for a **one-time $400 home office equipment stipend** during their first year of employment to buy a desk chair, monitor, docking station, or webcam. This benefit does not renew on a multi-year cycle and unused balances expire on the employee's one-year anniversary. Any hardware purchased through `RW-ERG-400` must be tagged by IT and returned to IT Asset Management via prepaid shipping label upon separation from the company, regardless of employee tenure.

## Section 1.6: Security, Privacy, and Out-of-State Work (Code: RW-SEC-10)
Employees working remotely must connect through the corporate OpenVPN gateway whenever accessing internal staging, production, or customer-data environments. Shared living spaces or coworking desks must use physical privacy screen filters on company laptops. Remote work from outside an employee's primary state of tax residence is limited to **5 business days per calendar year** with advance payroll notice, and international remote work is strictly prohibited without prior executive board approval. Violation of these guidelines may lead to disciplinary review.
