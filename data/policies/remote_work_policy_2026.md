# Northstar Cloud Corp. — Hybrid & Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-RW-2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 (Version 2.1)  
**Owner:** People Operations & Workplace Experience

## Section 1.1: Purpose and Eligibility
Northstar Cloud Corp. supports flexible hybrid work arrangements to balance focused individual productivity with in-person team collaboration across our engineering, product, and corporate operations organizations. All full-time regular employees who have completed their initial 60-day onboarding period and maintain satisfactory performance standing are eligible for hybrid remote work. Contractors, interns, and role-critical on-site facilities or hardware lab staff follow separate site attendance schedules specified in their engagement agreements or offer letters.

## Section 1.2: Weekly Remote Work Cadence (Code: RW-101)
Eligible employees may work remotely up to **three (3) days per week**, with Tuesdays and Wednesdays designated as anchor collaboration days at their assigned regional office. Team leads may approve a temporary exception for up to four remote days during critical sprint deadlines, family emergencies, or severe weather advisories. Requests for permanent fully remote status require joint written approval from a Department Vice President and People Operations. Employees working remotely must maintain an accurate work-location indicator in the company directory by 9:30 AM each morning for workplace safety and space-planning records.

## Section 1.3: Core Collaboration Hours and Availability (Code: RW-102)
To support cross-functional coordination across distributed teams, all hybrid employees must observe **core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their primary work time zone**, Monday through Friday. During core hours, employees are expected to remain reachable on Slack, respond to direct messages within 30 minutes when not in a scheduled meeting, and attend video calls with cameras enabled for team ceremonies and sprint reviews. Flexible start and end times outside core hours are encouraged as long as total weekly capacity meets role expectations and scheduled customer commitments are respected.

## Section 1.4: Home Internet Stipend (Code: RW-NET-75)
Northstar Cloud Corp. reimburses eligible hybrid and remote employees up to **$75 per month** toward residential high-speed internet service. To qualify, connections must provide at least 100 Mbps download and 20 Mbps upload bandwidth to support reliable video conferencing and cloud builds. Claims are submitted monthly through ConcurFlow using billing code `RW-NET-75` with a PDF statement showing the service address and billing period. Stipends are non-cumulative, cannot be applied toward mobile phone family plans, and must be submitted within 30 days of the statement date.

## Section 1.5: Ergonomic Home Office Allowance (Code: RW-ERG-600)
Upon completing 60 days of service, each eligible employee receives a **$600 ergonomic equipment allowance every three (3) years** to purchase an adjustable chair, standing desk converter, external monitor, or ergonomic keyboard and mouse setup. Purchases over $250 require pre-approval from the employee's direct manager in Workday before ordering. Equipment purchased under `RW-ERG-600` remains company property if the employee departs within 12 months of reimbursement; after 12 months of continuous service, ownership transfers cleanly to the employee.

## Section 1.6: Workspace Security and International Travel (Code: RW-SEC-10)
Remote workspaces must use a password-protected private Wi-Fi network (WPA2 or WPA3); public coffee shop, airport, or hotel networks require an active corporate WireGuard VPN tunnel at all times. Employees must lock their laptop screens whenever stepping away and store printed confidential documents in a locked drawer. Working remotely from outside an employee's country of employment is capped at **15 business days per calendar year** and requires prior tax, export-control, and security clearance from People Operations at least 14 days before departure. Non-compliance with security controls may result in temporary revocation of remote privileges.
