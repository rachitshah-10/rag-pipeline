# Northstar Cloud Corp. — Paid Time Off & Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-PTO-2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 (Version 2.4)  
**Owner:** People Operations & Benefits

## Section 3.1: Annual PTO Accrual Tiers (Code: PTO-101)
Full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) on a biweekly basis according to continuous tenure with Northstar Cloud Corp.:
- **0 to 2 years of service:** 15 days (120 hours) per year.
- **3 to 5 years of service:** 20 days (160 hours) per year.
- **6 or more years of service:** 25 days (200 hours) per year.

Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rated schedule. New hires begin accruing PTO on their first day of employment and may take accrued days immediately upon manager approval. Requests of five consecutive days or longer should be submitted in Workday at least two weeks in advance so teams can plan sprint and on-call coverage without disruption.

## Section 3.2: Year-End Carryover Cap and Payouts (Code: PTO-102)
To encourage regular rest throughout the year, employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days (40 hours)** from one calendar year to the next past December 31. Any unused balance exceeding 5 days is forfeited on January 1 unless an exception was granted in writing by People Operations due to a company-mandated project freeze or critical product launch. Where required by state law (such as California), accrued PTO vests without forfeiture and is capped at 1.5 times the employee's annual accrual limit. Unused PTO is paid out upon separation only where mandated by applicable state statute.

## Section 3.3: Paid Sick and Wellness Leave (Code: PTO-201)
Separate from vacation PTO, full-time employees receive **8 paid sick and wellness days (64 hours)** credited upfront on January 1 each year (pro-rated by hire month for mid-year hires). Sick leave covers personal illness, preventive medical or dental appointments, mental health recovery days, and caring for an immediate family member. Absences exceeding three consecutive workdays may require a healthcare provider's return-to-work note submitted confidentially to People Operations. Unused sick days do not carry over across calendar years and are not paid out upon termination.

## Section 3.4: Paid Parental Leave (Code: PTO-301)
Northstar Cloud Corp. provides **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave** for all regular full-time employees following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child, regardless of primary or secondary caregiver status. Parental leave may be taken continuously or split into two distinct blocks within 12 months of the qualifying event. During paid parental leave, health benefits, equity vesting, and PTO accruals continue uninterrupted. Employees must notify People Operations at least 30 days prior to the expected leave start date when foreseeable so coverage plans can be coordinated smoothly.

## Section 3.5: Bereavement, Jury Duty, and Company Holidays (Code: PTO-302)
Employees receive up to **10 paid bereavement days** for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling) and **5 paid days** for extended family such as grandparents, aunts, uncles, or in-laws. Paid jury duty leave is provided for the full duration of required court service upon submitting the official court summons in Workday. In addition, Northstar Cloud Corp. observes 11 paid corporate holidays annually plus a company-wide winter recharge shutdown between December 26 and December 31 for all eligible staff.
