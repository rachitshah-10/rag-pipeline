# Northstar Cloud Corp. — Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-EXP-2026  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026 (Version 3.0)  
**Owner:** Finance & Corporate Accounting

## Section 2.1: General Principles and Approval Authority
Northstar Cloud Corp. reimburses employees for reasonable, documented business expenses incurred while performing authorized company duties. Employees are expected to exercise prudent judgment and spend company funds as carefully as personal funds. All travel exceeding $1,500 in total estimated cost requires pre-trip manager approval in ConcurFlow before booking airfare or lodging. Unapproved trips or unsubstantiated charges may be delayed or declined during Finance audit review.

## Section 2.2: Domestic and International Meal Per Diem (Code: EXP-301)
When traveling overnight on company business, employees are reimbursed up to **$75 per day for domestic meals** and **$95 per day for international meals**, inclusive of taxes and up to a 20% gratuity. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner receipts must be itemized showing every food and beverage line item; credit card summary slips without line-item details will be rejected by Finance. Unused daily meal per diems do not roll over to subsequent travel days. Team lunches for local non-traveling staff require a pre-approved team-building budget and are capped at $30 per person.

## Section 2.3: Client Entertainment and Hospitality (Code: EXP-302)
Business meals or hospitality events with external customers, partners, or prospects are governed by code `EXP-302` and capped at **$150 per attendee**, including tax and tip. Any client entertainment event estimated above $500 total or involving alcohol exceeding two drinks per guest requires **written pre-approval from a Department Vice President** uploaded to the expense packet. Every `EXP-302` submission must list the full names, job titles, and company affiliations of all external and internal attendees along with the business purpose discussed to satisfy corporate tax audit requirements.

## Section 2.4: Airfare, Rail, and Lodging Standards (Code: EXP-303)
Flights under six hours must be booked in standard economy or main-cabin class at least 14 days prior to departure whenever feasible. Premium economy upgrades are permitted only on international flights exceeding eight hours of continuous airtime. Regional rail travel (such as Amtrak Acela or Eurostar standard business) is encouraged for intercity corridors under four hours when cost-competitive with airfare. Hotel accommodations should be booked through the corporate travel portal at standard business rates, targeting **$275 per night before taxes** in major metropolitan hubs (New York, San Francisco, London) and $200 per night in other cities.

## Section 2.5: Receipt Submission Window and Payouts (Code: EXP-401)
All expense reports and itemized receipts must be submitted in ConcurFlow within **30 calendar days** of the transaction date or trip return date, whichever is later. Missing-receipt affidavits are accepted only twice per calendar year for individual charges under $50. Reports submitted between 31 and 60 days require Finance Controller sign-off; expenses older than 60 days are ineligible for reimbursement. Approved reports are paid via direct deposit in the next biweekly payroll cycle.

## Section 2.6: Non-Reimbursable Expenses (Code: EXP-402)
Under code `EXP-402`, the following items are strictly non-reimbursable: stand-alone bar tabs or alcohol-only receipts without a meal, first-class or business-class airfare without a documented medical or VP exception, personal gym or spa fees, in-room movie rentals, traffic or parking fines, lost luggage replacement beyond airline coverage, and personal airline lounge memberships. Corporate cards may never be used for personal purchases, even if the employee intends to repay the balance immediately; repeated misuse may result in card suspension.
